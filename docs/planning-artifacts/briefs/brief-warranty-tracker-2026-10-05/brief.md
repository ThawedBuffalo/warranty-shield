# Product Brief: Warranty Shield

## Executive Summary

**Warranty Shield** is a warranty tracking application that prevents people from losing money on forgotten warranties. It combines **smart expiration notifications** with **fast, zero-friction data capture** — solving the two biggest barriers to warranty tracking: forgetting about warranties and the hassle of entering warranty details.

The product targets everyday consumers who buy products with warranties (electronics, appliances, clothing, software) and want peace of mind without the administrative burden. Instead of managing a spreadsheet or hoping they'll remember to file a claim, users get a simple, beautiful experience that tells them exactly what's covered, when coverage expires, and what to do next.

**Why now?** Consumers are increasingly aware of the financial value of warranties, yet no product has made tracking them effortless. With the rise of e-commerce (more purchases = more warranties), subscription models, and the growing secondhand market (where transferable warranties add value), the timing is right for a simple, focused solution.

## The Problem

People lose hundreds — sometimes thousands — of dollars annually on warranties they didn't know they had, or claims they filed late because they forgot.

**Real scenarios:**
- A refrigerator breaks 11 months after purchase. The warranty expired 30 days ago. The user pays $800 out of pocket.
- A family buys a new laptop, TV, and washing machine during a holiday sale. Six months later, the TV stops working. They never registered the warranty. The claim is denied.
- Someone inherits a parent's electronics. They have no idea what's covered, what expired, and what documents exist.

**How people cope today:**
- They write dates on sticky notes (which fall off).
- They dig through email inboxes searching for purchase confirmations (which are buried under hundreds of other emails).
- They keep physical warranty cards in a drawer (which gets lost during moves).
- They simply accept the loss as "the cost of doing business."

**The cost of the status quo** is real money lost, frustration, and a sense of powerlessness over their own purchases.

## The Solution

Warranty Shield gives users a **single, beautiful dashboard** that shows every warranty they own, color-coded by status (green = safe, yellow = expiring soon, red = expired or critical), with proactive notifications that tell them exactly when to act.

**The experience:**
1. **Add a warranty in seconds** — through a fast manual entry form, by scanning a receipt with their phone camera, or by letting the app auto-capture warranty info from their purchase confirmation emails.
2. **See everything at a glance** — a clean dashboard shows all warranties, sorted by urgency. Green means you're covered. Yellow means time to act. Red means a claim window has passed.
3. **Get smart notifications** — configurable reminders at 90, 60, 30, and 7 days before expiration. The app suggests: "Your washing machine warranty expires in 14 days. If it breaks, here's how to file a claim."
4. **Keep documents organized** — attach receipts, warranty cards, and manuals as PDFs or photos. Everything lives with the warranty, searchable and always accessible.

**The outcome:** Users stop losing money on forgotten warranties. They feel confident about their purchases. They save money — and the app shows them exactly how much.

## What Makes This Different

| Competitor Approach | Warranty Shield Approach |
|---|---|
| Manual data entry is the norm | **Zero-friction capture** — email parsing, receipt scanning, fast forms |
| Generic calendar reminders | **Smart, configurable notifications** — 90/60/30/7 day alerts, tailored per warranty |
| A list of expired warranties | **Color-coded urgency dashboard** — green/yellow/red at a glance |
| One user, one account | **Family sharing built in** — track warranties for the whole household |
| A utility nobody enjoys using | **The "aha" moment** — "You've saved $2,340 on claims" transforms it from chore to superpower |

**The unfair advantage:** Execution speed and focus. We're not building an e-commerce platform, a loyalty program, or an insurance marketplace. We're building the best warranty tracking experience, period. The moat is **user habit formation** — once people trust Warranty Shield to protect their purchases, switching costs are emotional, not technical.

## Who This Serves

**Primary users:**
- **Homeowners and renters** (ages 25-55) who regularly purchase electronics, appliances, and home goods with warranties. They're digitally comfortable but time-poor. They've lost money on a forgotten warranty at least once and want to prevent it from happening again.
- **What success looks like:** They add a warranty in under 30 seconds, never miss an expiration, and feel confident their purchases are protected.

**Secondary users:**
- **Gift recipients** — someone receives a product with a warranty and wants it tracked without the hassle.
- **Families** — one person manages warranties for multiple household members (spouse, kids, aging parents).
- **People selling used items** — knowing the remaining warranty time boosts resale value.

## Success Criteria

**User success signals:**
- **Activation:** 70% of users add at least 3 warranties within their first week
- **Retention:** 60% of users return to the app at least once per month (driven by notifications and dashboard checks)
- **Value perception:** 50% of users report "saving money" within their first 6 months (tracked via the warranty value calculator)
- **Growth:** 30% of users invite at least one family member (family sharing is the viral lever)

**Business objectives:**
- Reach 10,000 active users within 12 months of launch
- Maintain <5% monthly churn rate
- Achieve 4.5+ star rating on app stores
- Validate willingness to pay for premium features (subscription or freemium) before pursuing Phase 4 monetization

## Scope

**In for Version 1 (MVP):**
- Mobile app (iOS and Android) with manual warranty entry wizard
- Smart expiration notifications (configurable: 90/60/30/7 days before expiration)
- Color-coded dashboard (green/yellow/red status)
- Email integration — parse purchase confirmation emails to auto-capture warranty info
- Basic document upload — attach receipts, warranty cards, manuals (PDFs or photos)
- Cloud backup with account recovery

**Out of Version 1:**
- OCR receipt scanning (Phase 2)
- Barcode/QR scanning (Phase 2)
- Family sharing (Phase 2)
- AI-powered features (Phase 3)
- Retailer integrations (Phase 4)
- Subscription or marketplace features (Phase 4)

**Why this scope:** The MVP solves the core problem — preventing money loss from forgotten warranties — without requiring partnerships, ML models, or complex integrations. It delivers value on day one.

## Vision

If Warranty Shield succeeds, it becomes the **default layer of financial protection** between consumers and their purchases — a trusted utility that sits quietly in the background, alerting users only when it matters.

**In 2-3 years:**
- Every major retailer auto-pushes warranty data to Warranty Shield (Amazon, Best Buy, Apple, etc.)
- Warranty data becomes a factor in the secondhand marketplace — buyers check "remaining warranty" before purchasing used goods
- Warranty Shield offers bundled protection plans at expiration, partnering with insurers
- The platform becomes the **source of truth** for product warranty status across the consumer economy

But none of that matters if the MVP doesn't work. The vision starts with one simple promise: **never let a warranty expire without you knowing.**
