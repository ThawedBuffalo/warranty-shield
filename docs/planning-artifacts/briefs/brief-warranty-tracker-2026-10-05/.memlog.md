- (event) Session started: creating product brief from prior brainstorming session
- (decision) Input: brainstorming session at docs/brainstorming/brainstorm-intent.md with 50 ideas across 8 clusters, prioritized via Affinity Clustering + Impact-Effort
- (decision) Product name: Warranty Shield — a warranty tracking application that prevents users from losing money on forgotten warranties
- (gap) Core problem: people lose money on warranties because they forget about them. No existing solution combines smart notifications with fast data capture.
- (insight) Key insights: (1) notifications + email capture solve onboarding, (2) family sharing creates viral growth, (3) warranty value calculator is the retention 'aha' moment
- (decision) MVP scope: smart expiration notifications, fast manual entry wizard, color-coded dashboard, email capture from purchase confirmations, basic document upload
- (decision) Primary users: consumers who buy products with warranties (electronics, appliances, clothing) and want to avoid losing money on forgotten coverage
- (insight) Differentiator: combines smart notifications with fast zero-friction data capture — no other product does both at this simplicity level
- (decision) Product brief created at docs/planning-artifacts/briefs/brief-warranty-tracker-2026-10-05/brief.md — 7 sections: Executive Summary, Problem, Solution, Differentiation, Users, Success Criteria, Scope, Vision
